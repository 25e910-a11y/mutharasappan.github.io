02

River Waste Capture and Pollution Source Localisation Design | 1st place

Sparkathon 2.0, Concetto'26, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad | Team PSG CT 2

Cleanup usually starts after the waste is already in the river. We designed a network that catches plastic safely and also points to the stretch of river it is coming from.

  • Collector node with a camera and on-device model. Waste is diverted; fish and anything the model isn't sure about pass through.
  • Floating walls let fish escape underneath, with a bubble, sound and LED barrier to steer them away.
  • Source tracing borrowed from power lines: like a travelling-wave fault locator, waste counts, flow and travel time between nodes rank the likely source segments.
  • Low-cost solar sensor nodes send data over LoRa to a gateway, server and map dashboard for authorities.

Load management for Time-of-Day tariffs
