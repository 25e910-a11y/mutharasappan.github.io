River waste capture and source tracing

A network of river nodes that catches plastic without harming fish, and points to the stretch of river the waste is coming from.

  • Design, won 1st place
  • Sparkathon 2.0, Concetto'26, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, October 2026
  • Team PSG CT 2, with my classmate D K Mosi Keeran

The problem

River cleanup usually starts after the waste is already in the river. Barriers and nets can catch some of it, but they can trap fish too, and they don't tell anyone where the waste entered the water. Without knowing the source, the same stretch keeps getting dirty.

How it works

  • Collector node. A camera with an on-device model looks at what is coming downstream. Waste is diverted into the collector. Fish, and anything the model isn't sure about, pass through.
  • Fish-safe barrier. Floating walls let fish escape underneath, with a bubble, sound and LED barrier to steer them away.
  • Source tracing. The idea comes from power lines. A travelling-wave fault locator finds a fault from the time a wave takes to reach each end. Here, waste counts, water flow and travel time between nodes are used the same way, to rank the river stretches most likely to be the source.

Data path

  1. Sensor nodes
  2. LoRa
  3. Gateway
  4. Server
  5. Map dashboard

The nodes run on solar power with MPPT charging, so they can sit on the river without a power line.

Parts and tools

ESP32-S3, LoRa, MQTT, YOLO-nano, solar panel with MPPT charge controller.

Result

The design won 1st place at Sparkathon 2.0, part of Concetto'26 at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. At the same fest, I also won 3rd place in Aeroglide.
