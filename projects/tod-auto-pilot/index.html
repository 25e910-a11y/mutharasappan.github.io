ToD Auto-Pilot

A device that moves loads that can wait into the cheaper hours of a Time-of-Day tariff, to bring the electricity bill down.

  • Built, version 2
  • Load management
  • PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore

The problem

Under a Time-of-Day (ToD) tariff, the same unit of electricity costs more at peak hours and less at off-peak hours. Many loads don't need to run at a fixed time: a pump filling a tank, a water heater, a battery charger. If they run at peak hours by habit, the bill is higher than it needs to be.

What it does

  • Keeps the time of day with a real-time clock, so it knows which tariff period it is in.
  • Measures the power each load draws with a PZEM-004T energy meter module.
  • Switches the loads that can wait into the cheaper hours through SSRs, relays and contactors.

Version 2 monitors and controls three loads at a time.
